📌 Project Overview

The Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to monitor and analyze supply chain performance, revenue, profitability, inventory levels, and supplier efficiency.

The dashboard provides an interactive and visual overview of key supply chain metrics, helping businesses identify inventory risks, monitor regional performance, and make data-driven operational decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and gross margin.
- Compare regional sales and profitability.
- Monitor inventory health and identify low-stock items.
- Evaluate supplier lead times.
- Track monthly revenue trends.
- Identify potential supply chain risks.

📈 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

1. Revenue and Gross Margin by Region

- Compares revenue and gross margin across East, North, South, and West regions.
- Helps evaluate regional business performance.

2. SKU Count by Risk Category

- Displays inventory distribution across risk categories.
- Helps identify healthy inventory and low-stock products.

3. Average Supplier Lead Time

- Compares average delivery lead times across suppliers.
- Helps evaluate supplier performance and delivery efficiency.

4. Monthly Revenue Trend

- Tracks revenue performance throughout the year.
- Identifies monthly growth patterns, declines, and recovery trends.

🔍 Key Insights

- Total revenue reached 33.43M.
- Gross margin contributed 11.09M.
- A total of 1.83M units were sold.
- 5,041 items were identified as low stock.
- March recorded the highest monthly revenue in the displayed trend.
- Regional revenue performance was relatively consistent across the four regions.
- Supplier lead times vary, highlighting opportunities for supply chain optimization.
